# AI Implementation Plan

## 1. Overview  
**Objective:** Deliver a repeatable, risk‑aware AI rollout that aligns with business goals and scales efficiently.  
**Scope:** 6‑phase roadmap (Strategic Planning → Pilot → Scale → Optimize) with embedded checklists, KPIs, and risk checkpoints.

---

## 2. Header

| Item | Details |
|------|---------|
| **Strategic Goal** | *e.g.* “Reduce customer churn by 15 % within 12 months.” |
| **Use‑Case Prioritization** | 3–5 high‑impact, high‑feasibility pilots |
| **Timeline** | 18 months (Q1‑Q6 2027) |
| **Budget** | $2 M (R&D + Ops) |
| **Team** | 12 members (Data Science, Engineering, Ops, Business) |
| **Stakeholders** | C‑suite, Product, Ops, Legal, Compliance |

---

## 3. Roadmap Phases

| Phase | Duration | Key Milestones | Owners | Risks & Mitigations |
|-------|----------|----------------|--------|---------------------|
| **1. Strategic Planning** | Q1 2027 | • Business charter<br>• Use‑case matrix<br>• KPI definition | PM, PMO, Product Lead | • Scope creep – enforce charter <br>• Misaligned KPIs – double‑check with Finance |
| **2. Data & Infra Prep** | Q1‑Q2 2027 | • Data inventory & quality audit<br>• Data lake build<br>• Compute allocation | Data Engineering | • Data silos – cross‑team sync<br>• Security gaps – audit with Security |
| **3. Model Development** | Q2‑Q3 2027 | • Baseline model prototypes<br>• Validation & bias checks<br>• Feature store | Data Science, ML Ops | • Model drift – set drift detection<br>• Over‑fitting – cross‑validation |
| **4. Pilot Deployment** | Q3 2027 | • MVP in sandbox<br>• A/B testing<br>• Stakeholder review | ML Ops, Product | • Low adoption – include UX review<br>• Latency – monitor infra |
| **5. Scale & Integration** | Q4 2027 – Q1 2028 | • Full‑stack integration<br>• CI/CD pipeline<br>• Monitoring dashboards | Engineering, Ops | • Cost spikes – set budgets<br>• Compliance – continuous audit |
| **6. Optimization & Governance** | Q2 2028 – Q3 2028 | • Model retraining schedule<br>• Governance policy rollout<br>• ROI analysis | PMO, Legal | • Governance gaps – quarterly reviews |

## 5. Key Metrics (KPIs)

|-----|--------|-------------|
